TOEIC Part 5 Practice: provided that

PCでは「問題 → 解説」を分離して表示します。

Q.1

Choose the option that best completes the sentence.

Provided that the revised budget proposal _____ by the board, the project timeline will remain unchanged.

  • A. approves
  • B. is approved
  • C. has approved
  • D. will approve

Correct Answer: B   (is approved)

Explanation

“Provided that” introduces a condition clause, so the present tense is used even for future meaning. The subject requires a passive form.

解説

「provided that」は条件節のため現在形を用います。 主語は承認される側なので受動態が必要です。
