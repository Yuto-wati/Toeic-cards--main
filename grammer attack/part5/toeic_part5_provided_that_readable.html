TOEIC Part 5 Practice: provided that

Exam-level (Q1–Q3)

Questions & choices are English-only. Explanations include English + Japanese “解説”.

Q.1

Choose the option that best completes the sentence.

Provided that the revised budget proposal _____ by the board, the project timeline will remain unchanged.

  • A. approves
  • B. is approved
  • C. has approved
  • D. will approve
Correct Answer: B is approved

Explanation

“Provided that” introduces a condition clause, so the present tense is used even when the main clause refers to the future. Since the subject is the proposal itself, a passive form is required.

解説

「provided that」は条件節を導く表現で、未来の内容であっても現在形を用います。 また、主語は「承認される側」なので受動態が必要です。よって正解は is approved です。

Q.2

Choose the option that best completes the sentence.

The discount will be applied at checkout, provided that the coupon code _____ before the expiration date.

  • A. enters
  • B. is entered
  • C. has been entered
  • D. will be entering
Correct Answer: B is entered

Explanation

The coupon code is something that is entered, so a passive form is needed. In condition clauses with “provided that,” the present tense is used.

解説

coupon code は「入力される側」なので受動態が必要です。 また条件節のため will は使えません。正解は is entered です。

Q.3

Choose the option that best completes the sentence.

Provided that no additional documentation _____ during the review process, approval can be granted within five business days.

  • A. requires
  • B. is required
  • C. will require
  • D. has required
Correct Answer: B is required

Explanation

“Documentation” is uncountable and the meaning is passive. Condition clauses avoid using “will.”

解説

documentation は不可算名詞で、意味も受動です。条件節のため現在形を用い、正解は is required となります。

Note

Tip: In condition clauses (provided that / if / as long as), avoid “will” and focus on voice (active vs. passive).
